About Honeyland School

Honeyland School is a well-established Nursery, Primary, and Secondary School located in Ipaja, Lagos State. We provide both Day and Boarding facilities in a safe, disciplined, and nurturing environment. Our goal is to develop confident, intelligent, and morally upright students prepared for future success.

Our Programs

Nursery School

Early childhood education focused on foundational learning and creativity.

Primary School

Strong academic foundation with emphasis on literacy, numeracy, and discipline.

Secondary School

Comprehensive curriculum preparing students for WAEC, NECO, and future careers.

Our Facilities

Boarding House

Modern Classrooms

Well-ventilated and equipped classrooms for effective learning.

Playground

Spacious environment for sports and recreational activities.

Admissions

Admissions are open for Nursery, Primary, and Secondary classes. Interested parents and guardians are encouraged to contact us or visit the school for more information.
